Booking Guide

Last updated: October 2026

A smoother booking, from start to finish.

Please review these booking guidelines before requesting a FURLUXE grooming appointment. They help us prepare for your pet and provide a safe, comfortable and organized experience.

01 Appointment Confirmation

An appointment request submitted through our website is a request for a preferred date and time. It does not automatically guarantee an appointment.

Your appointment should be considered confirmed only after FURLUXE communicates confirmation to you.

Please keep your phone number and contact information accurate so our team can reach you regarding your booking.

02 Arrival & Appointment Timing

Please arrive at the agreed appointment time. Arriving on time helps us maintain the schedule and provide each pet with appropriate attention.

Arrive at the scheduled time.
Keep your pet safely secured while arriving.
Bring any requested information about your pet.
Inform us if you expect to be significantly late.

03 Cancellation & Rescheduling

If you need to cancel or change your appointment, please contact FURLUXE as early as possible.

Early communication gives our team the opportunity to adjust the schedule and make the appointment slot available to another customer.

Any applicable cancellation or rescheduling policy will be communicated to you before a charge is applied.

04 Missed Appointments

If an appointment is missed without prior communication, the appointment slot may no longer be available.

Repeated missed appointments may affect future booking availability or may require additional confirmation before another appointment is scheduled.

05 Pet Information

Please provide accurate information about your pet when making a booking. This helps our grooming team understand your pet's needs before the appointment.

  • Pet name and basic details.
  • Breed or breed type, where known.
  • Approximate size and coat condition.
  • Requested grooming service.
  • Relevant behavioural or handling information.

06 Health & Safety

Please inform the grooming team about any known allergies, skin sensitivities, injuries, medical restrictions, behavioural concerns or other conditions that could affect grooming.

If a requested service appears unsuitable for the pet's current condition, the grooming team may recommend modifying, postponing or declining the service.

Your pet's comfort and safety should always come first. Please share important information with our team before grooming begins.

07 Grooming Services

The final grooming service may depend on your pet's coat, size, breed, temperament, condition and individual grooming requirements.

A requested style or result may not always be possible if it would be unsuitable or uncomfortable for the pet.

Any significant change to the requested service should be discussed with the customer before proceeding where reasonably possible.

08 Pricing & Payment

Grooming prices may vary based on the selected service, package and the individual requirements of your pet.

Website pricing is subject to change. The applicable price should be confirmed during the booking or service process.

If additional grooming work is required, the applicable additional cost should be communicated before proceeding whenever reasonably possible.

09 Customer Responsibility

By requesting an appointment, customers agree to provide accurate information and follow reasonable instructions relating to their pet's visit.

Customers should also ensure that their pet can be safely transported to and from the grooming location.

These booking terms do not remove or limit any rights or protections that cannot legally be excluded or limited under applicable law.

10 Questions About Your Booking?

If you have questions about an appointment, grooming service, cancellation or any booking requirement, please contact the FURLUXE team before submitting your request.

Ready to book?

Now that you know how our booking process works, you can request a grooming appointment for your pet.
