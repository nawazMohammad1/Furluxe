OUR STORY

Where care meets luxury.

FURLUXE was created with one simple belief: grooming should feel as good as it looks. A calm environment, thoughtful care and a beautiful experience — for pets and their people.

WHY FURLUXE

Because every pet deserves to feel special.

We noticed something simple: pet grooming is often treated as a task, when it can be so much more.

FURLUXE was imagined as a place where grooming becomes an experience — one built around patience, comfort, cleanliness and attention to detail.

From the first hello to the final little tail wag, every part of the experience is designed to make pets feel comfortable and cared for.

“Beautiful grooming starts with making them feel safe.”

— The FURLUXE Philosophy

THE NAME

Why we call it FURLUXE.

“Fur” represents the pets at the heart of everything we do. “Luxe” represents the elevated experience we want every pet and pet parent to enjoy.

Together, FURLUXE represents our idea of premium pet grooming without losing the warmth, friendliness and genuine care that pets deserve.
