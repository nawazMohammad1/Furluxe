Your information, handled with care.

This Privacy Policy describes how FURLUXE collects, uses and protects information that you provide when using our website, contacting us or requesting pet grooming services.

01 Information We Collect

When you interact with FURLUXE, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, contact details, pet information and appointment preferences.

Depending on how you use our website, information may include:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Pet name, breed and grooming requirements.
  • Appointment preferences and service requests.
  • Messages or enquiries submitted through our contact forms.

02 How We Use Information

Information you provide may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange appointments and provide the grooming services you request.

We may also use information to improve our website, communicate with you about your requests and provide relevant service information.

FURLUXE aims to collect only the information reasonably needed to communicate with you and manage requested services.

03 Appointments & Enquiries

If you submit an appointment request, the information you provide may be used to understand your preferred service, schedule and pet-care needs.

FURLUXE may contact you using the contact information you provide in order to confirm details, respond to questions or discuss changes relating to your request.

04 Cookies & Website Usage

Our website may use basic browser technologies such as cookies or similar mechanisms where necessary for website functionality.

These technologies can help a website remember preferences, understand general usage and provide a smoother browsing experience.

05 Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with FURLUXE from unauthorized access, misuse or unnecessary disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06 Your Choices

You can choose what information you provide when contacting FURLUXE. Some information may be necessary for us to respond to an enquiry or arrange a requested appointment.

If you have questions about information you have shared with us, please contact FURLUXE so we can discuss your request.

07 Changes to This Policy

FURLUXE may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website, services or practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised update date.

08 Contact FURLUXE

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact our team through the FURLUXE Contact page.

Have a privacy question?

Send us your enquiry and our team will be happy to help with questions about your information or our privacy practices.
